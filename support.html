Support

For BrainTrain and BrainTrain Kids.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open an issue on GitHub. Please mention which app, your iPhone model and iOS version.

Restore a purchase

BrainTrain: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.
BrainTrain Kids: tap the grown-ups button, answer the question, then tap Restore purchase.

Cancel a subscription

BrainTrain Plus is billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose BrainTrain. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Family Sharing

The BrainTrain Kids full unlock can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Progress

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes it. It is not synced between devices.

Common questions

A game is locked. Free games are open up to a set level. BrainTrain Plus (adults) or the full unlock (kids) opens every game and level.

No sound or vibration. Check the switches in the app's settings, and that your iPhone isn't in silent mode.
