Privacy Policy

BrainTrain and BrainTrain Kids · Last updated 12 October 2026

This policy covers the iOS apps BrainTrain and BrainTrain Kids ("the apps").

What we collect

Nothing. The apps do not collect, send to a server, or share any personal information. They have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking or SDKs.

What stays on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, personal bests and stickers) and settings (sound and haptics) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

BrainTrain Kids is made for children aged 6 to 8 and is part of the App Store Kids Category. It collects no data from children. Purchases and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups gate.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
